# Block or Talk: demo video script (T-Mobile Safe Signal track)

About 2½ minutes. The narrator is ElevenLabs "Chris". Yui, Ren and Kai use the same voices as their live agents (Matilda, Bella, Eric). The player, Alex, uses "River". The video is recorded from the real app running locally. The calls run in text mode, and each line is read aloud in its caller's voice. The Carrier insights numbers come from the synthetic demo dataset (`npm run insights:seed`), so describe them as illustrative.

| # | Screen | Audio |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Landing page `/` | **Narrator:** Scam calls don't sound like scams anymore. The caller knows your name, your bank, even your last purchase. Block or Talk lets you practice that moment, before it's real. |
| 2 | Challenge board `/challenge` | **Narrator:** This is a Challenge day. You start with a thousand dollars in fictional money and two everyday tasks: book a job interview, and sort out a bank alert. Three AI callers will phone you. Some are real. Some are scams. You decide who gets your trust. |
